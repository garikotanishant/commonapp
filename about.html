WHY COMMON EXISTS

A place to find
your people.
And your way.

✳

Every community has good things.
They just aren’t always easy to find.

We built COMMON to bring a little of that local knowledge together. A school for your family. A park for your afternoon. A service for when life gets complicated.

MADE HERE, FOR HERE

Useful. Simple. Neighborly.

01 /

Start with a real question.

Search in everyday language or choose a category. You shouldn’t have to understand a department chart to find help.

02 /

Keep the details together.

Addresses, contacts, hours, eligibility, and source links live with each resource. Save what matters and come back when you’re ready.

03 /

Leave room to grow.

A good guide is never finished. Suggest a resource, flag an outdated detail, or tell us how the guide could be more useful.

SOURCES & THE SMALL PRINT

Good information starts
with a clear source.

Resource information

Listings draw on the community resource dataset and official school profile links. Hours, eligibility, and availability can change; check the source or call before visiting.

Community resource dataset (PDF) Official GCPS school directory

Maps, motion & your privacy

The interactive map uses Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. Globe geography uses Natural Earth via World Atlas, drawn with D3 and TopoJSON. Typography: DM Sans from Google Fonts.

Saved resources and suggestions stay in this browser. The guide does not automatically send them to the team. The map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap; your location is not requested.
